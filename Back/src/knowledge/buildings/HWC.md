# Highmark Center for Health, Wellness and Athletics

## Overview

The Highmark Center is a 160,000 square foot building recently opened which unifies robust health services, contemporary facilities and educational programs that support students’ minds, bodies and spirits in a comprehensive care model. It includes new full-service clinical areas as an expanded University Health Services; private and supportive spaces for Counseling and Psychological Services; and a modern athletics facility for practice, performance, training and athlete care.

Accuracy correction: the Highmark Center's cost is $105 million, not $200M+, per all official CMU sources. Groundbreaking was November 19, 2021; the building opened August 2024 for fall semester, with an official dedication October 24, 2024. 160,000 sq ft (142,000 new + 16,000 renovated) across four stories. Designed by Bohlin Cywinski Jackson, built in 36 months by Mascaro Construction, pursuing LEED Gold. The $35 million lead gift from Highmark, Inc. (Fall 2019) was one of the largest corporate partnerships in CMU history. Donor-named spaces include the Buncher Suite for Wellness, the Hoplamazian Family Well-Being Lab, the Rohr Intercollegiate Golf Training Facility, and the Block-Kelley Tartan Room. The building replaces the historic Skibo Gymnasium (1924), which hosted CMU's first basketball game (Feb. 5, 1924 vs. Pitt) and a Louis Armstrong performance. Skibo's core was preserved and woven into the new Center — its original exterior wall now lives inside as the secondary gym wall, 6,000 sq ft of salvaged wooden flooring appears in the Tartan Hall of Fame, the varsity weight room displays the original center-court Tartan logo, and copper feature walls are made from the patina-aged copper roofs of the old North Gym and Thistle Hall. Signature spaces: Main Arena (16,000 sq ft, seats ~1,000, home to varsity basketball and volleyball), secondary recreational gym (with six hoops, volleyball, three badminton courts, pickleball), Turf Fieldhouse inside Skibo's preserved shell (turf, running lanes, batting cages), Rohr Intercollegiate Golf Training Facility (three virtual training screens — used by the 2024 NCAA D-III Champion women's golf team), athletic training suite, student-athlete lounge. Upstairs: UHS clinic on the 3rd floor (with a juice bar), and CaPS counseling on the 4th floor with a Wellness Garden. The Buncher Suite includes four contemplation nooks, two ablution rooms, a gallery, and a circular Interfaith Room. UHS Wellness To Go vending machines provide on-site access to emergency contraception, sexual-health supplies, and OTC medications. Art includes Guadalupe Maravilla's Descansa Espíritus/Rest Spirits — six polished aluminum hammocks suspended as a symmetrical mobile, installed October 2024. Dining is Shake Smart — protein shakes, fruit smoothies, and açaí bowls. The Center is the first CMU facility to integrate physical health, mental health, spiritual life, and athletics under one roof, a model CMU positions as a national leader in holistic student wellness. It sits right next to Schenley Park.
