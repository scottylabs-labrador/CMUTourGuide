# Doherty Hall

## Overview

Doherty Hall contains some of the largest lecture style classrooms and renovated lab spaces for our Mellon College of Science students, in addition to lab and studio spaces for departments like Engineering, Chemistry, Biology and Art.

The Mellon College of Science houses 4 departments with 20 majors including biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics. Over 70% of undergraduates in this college do research. The college facilities also include a Cloud Lab that is the first academic Cloud Laboratory in the country and home to programmable robot-run experimentation instruments.

Also the new Richard King Mellon Hall of Sciences is coming in 2027 that will house our Mellon College of Science, the university's School of Computer Science and the public Institute for Contemporary Art. This mix of science, technology and art will spur exciting opportunities for cross-collaboration and embody Carnegie's Mellon spirit of innovation.

Built in 1908 as the School of Applied Sciences, Doherty anchors Hornbostel's "Mall" and houses Mellon College of Science's Chemistry and Biological Sciences departments alongside College of Engineering's Chemical Engineering (continuously there since 1908). Named for trustee Henry L. Doherty. The Phase II addition (2009, LEED Silver) added three green roofs, including a semi-intensive roof with a bluestone-and-beach-pebble spiral walkway above C-level that doubles as an outdoor classroom. Doherty's defining quirk: no two floors have identical layouts, you cannot walk from the front of the first floor to the back without detouring, and the third and fourth floors exist only in two small towers that don't connect to each other. The maze continues into Wean Hall via five different stair/hallway connections — a rite of passage for every first-year trying to find class. Five large interdisciplinary teaching labs serve chemistry and chem-E students; 90% of chemistry majors do undergraduate research. No dining inside; La Prima in Gates or Stephanie's in the Mellon Institute are closest. Clarifying the original brief: the CMU Cloud Lab is not located in Doherty — it's in a CMU-owned building near Bakery Square — but Doherty chemistry faculty are its primary users. In December 2023, Assistant Professor Gabe Gomes — with chemical engineering doctoral students Daniil Boiko and Robert MacKnight — published a Nature paper on "Coscientist," an AI system using GPT-4 and Anthropic's Claude that autonomously designed, planned, and executed chemistry experiments (including optimizing palladium-catalyzed cross-coupling reactions), leveraging the Cloud Lab's 200+ instruments running 24/7. Doherty's role will shift when the 338,900-sq-ft Richard King Mellon Hall of Sciences opens in 2027 — a roughly $300 million project (initial estimate $252M) anchored by a $75 million lead grant from the Richard King Mellon Foundation as part of a $150M partnership, designed by Portland's ZGF Architects with local R3A Architecture, with construction managed by a PJ Dick / Mascaro Construction joint venture; groundbreaking was April 12, 2024 at the corner of Forbes Avenue and South Craig Street, and the building will house the Mellon College of Science, parts of the School of Computer Science, and the Institute for Contemporary Art Pittsburgh.
