# Cohon University Center

## Overview

Jared L. Cohon University Center is our student union. Here students can take advantage of the student fitness facilities, catch a show at the black-box theater, visit the chapel, gather with other students in meeting rooms, or get lunch in one of many eateries.

You see our Scotty statue mascot that was funded and created by multiple CMU alumni. Our mascot is the Scottish Highland Terrier or Scottie for short. We like to call the dog “Scotty” who is a confident, independent and spirited breed. We students like to rub its nose for goodluck, so feel free to try that out before leaving this stop!

Additionally, located in this building is The Center for Student Diversity and Inclusion. The Center offers resources to enhance an inclusive and transformative student experience for our student population that joins us from 120 different countries. The Center is focused on access, success, campus climate and intergroup dialogue.

The Jared L. Cohon University Center is CMU's 24/7 student union, named in 2014 for 8th president Jared L. Cohon (1997–2013). Original building opened 1996 (Michael Dennis & Associates, postmodern echo of Hornbostel's Beaux-Arts); the 62,000-sq-ft expansion was designed by CannonDesign and built by Mosites Construction, opening May 2016 after a 19-month build, and winning the Master Builders' Association 2016 Building Excellence Award (New Construction $10–$25M) and an AIA Pennsylvania award. The new Lee Lobby features floor-to-ceiling windows, two native-plant rain gardens, and the signature art piece: Dale Chihuly's "Frank-Ratchye End of the Day Chandelier," a massive multi-colored blown-glass sculpture donated in 2013 by trustee Edward H. Frank and Sarah Ratchye to honor President Cohon, its colors symbolizing his commitment to diversity. Key spaces: McConomy Auditorium (445 seats, stadium-style), Rangos Ballroom (holds 450, dividable into three, hosts the Activities FAIR), Wiegand Gym, Maureen B. Cohon Cardio Fitness Center (10,000 sq ft), a 25-yard, 8-lane swimming pool (built 1996) with a separate diving well featuring two 1-meter and two 3-meter boards and a balcony seating up to 200 spectators, squash/racquetball courts, a Studio Theater black-box, a 24-hour multi-denominational prayer room, University Store, Art Store, and Tech Store. Dining is one of CMU's deepest lineups: Schatz Dining Room (campus's only all-you-care-to-eat buffet, 2nd floor), Au Bon Pain at Skibo Café, El Gallo de Oro (Mexican), Revolution Noodle (build-your-own Malatang noodle bowls, bao buns, bubble tea; opened 2022), Tazza D'Oro espresso, and Entropy+ convenience store. The Tartan Express food truck parks outside in Legacy Plaza. Outside in Merson Courtyard sits the bronze Scotty statue — CMU's Scottish terrier mascot — unveiled April 8, 2022 during Spring Carnival & Reunion Weekend, sculpted by alumnus Ray Kaskey (CFA) using a Scottish terrier named Bean as his model, and cast by Laran Bronze, a foundry owned by CFA alumnus Lawrence Welker. The statue's tail doubles as a handle so visitors can pull themselves up onto the granite base, which itself functions as a bench. It was funded by CFA alumna Kathy Sabec Dax and her late husband F. Robert Dax (a College of Engineering alumnus). The tradition is to rub Scotty's nose for good luck — and official CMU campus tours now conclude at the Scotty statue. The Scottish terrier became CMU's official mascot in 2007. The CUC hosts The FAIR (350+ student organizations), Late Night at CMU, orientation, and Spring Carnival events.
