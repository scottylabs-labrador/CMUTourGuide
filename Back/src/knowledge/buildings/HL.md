# Hunt Library

## Overview

Dedicated October 10, 1961, Hunt is CMU's main library and its only purpose-built library — the $2.8 million gift of Roy A. Hunt (an Alcoa founder, hence the aluminum-and-glass exterior) and his wife Rachel McMasters Miller Hunt, a legendary rare-book collector. Designed by Lawrie & Green, its cantilevered canopy names the donors on one side and the Rachel Hunt Botanical Library on the other, and nighttime LED illumination makes it glow from across The Cut. Five floors plus an A-level basement. The fifth-floor penthouse houses the Hunt Institute for Botanical Documentation — Rachel's personal rare-book legacy including medieval vellum manuscripts on medicinal herbs and Maria Sibylla Merian botanical books, "arguably the best collection of botanical books ever assembled by an individual in this country." The fourth floor has the Fine & Rare Book Room (600+ artists' books). The basement houses IDeATe — Integrative Design, Arts, and Technology — CMU's fabrication, media, physical computing, and soft technologies labs offering nine undergraduate minors fusing art and tech. The third floor is the dead-silent quiet floor, and the Hunt Library Gallery is currently hosting "Room to Imagine: 125 Years of Carnegie Mellon University" (Sept 11, 2025 – July 10, 2026) — curated by Julia Corrin with art director Heidi Wiren Kébé and editorial director Brad King — an immersive installation built into the form of a college dorm room and tracing CMU innovations from the iPhone interface (Freddy Anzures) to Toy Story (Ralph Guggenheim), Duolingo (Luis von Ahn), and Silly Putty (Earl Warrick), with a companion "Imagination to Innovation" section displaying toys designed by alumni. Dining is the Maggie Murph Café on the first floor, operated since fall 2022 by De Fer Coffee & Tea — a Pittsburgh specialty roastery founded in 2016 by CMU alumnus Matt Marietti (Tepper MBA '10) and his wife Vanessa, with six locations across Pittsburgh (flagship in the Strip District, where they roast in-house daily). Extended hours run Mon–Fri 8 a.m. to 3 a.m., and during finals weekends extend to 3 a.m. as well — valid CMU ID required after 7 p.m.
