# Tepper Quad

## Overview

Tepper Quad Building, is a 315,000 square foot state-of-the-art building, is home to the Tepper School of Business, the Coulter Welcome Center, fitness center, dining locations, JP Morgan and Chase AI Makerspace, and the Swartz Center for Entrepreneurship.

The Tepper School of Business houses three variations of undergraduate majors including Business administration major with 9 different concentrations, Multiple ways to earn an Economics major, and a Computational Finance major.

Here at CMU we do not have a central dining hall, but 30+ different eateries around campus. Inside the Tepper Building we have Rohr Commons featuring Build Pizza, Forbes Ave Subs, Tepper Taqueria, and Millie's Coffee ‘N Creamery.

Opened September 13, 2018, the David A. Tepper Quadrangle is CMU's largest building at 315,000 sq ft across five stories — each floor is over an acre. Designed by Moore Ruble Yudell Architects (lead: Buzz Yudell) with R3A as associate architect, and built by PJ Dick for roughly $201–205 million. Funded in part by a $67 million lead gift from alumnus David A. Tepper (TPR '82) toward the building itself — Tepper, a billionaire hedge-fund founder of Appaloosa Management and owner of the Carolina Panthers (NFL) and Charlotte FC (MLS), has given CMU more than $125 million in total, including $55 million to name and endow the Tepper School. Additional lead gifts: $5M from David Coulter (TPR '71) for the welcome center and $31M from Jim Swartz (TPR '66, co-founder of Accel Partners) for the entrepreneurship center. The building is LEED Gold (73 of 79 points) with 82,000 sq ft of exterior glass giving 85% of the interior natural light, and is the first BubbleDeck building in Pennsylvania and the largest-floor-area BubbleDeck installation in the United States — the recycled-plastic balls suspended in the concrete are reportedly made from rejected plastic kayaks, reducing concrete use by 35% and embodied carbon by ~30%. A 120,000-gallon rainwater cistern serves building systems. Notable spaces: Simmons Auditorium (600 seats, CMU's largest and flat-floor reconfigurable), the Swartz Center for Entrepreneurship (15,000 sq ft with 10 startup "maker-space garages"), a 7,200-sq-ft fitness center, the JPMorgan Chase AI Maker Space, the Askwith Kenner Global Languages Room, and 24 tech-enabled classrooms. The Coulter Welcome Center is where every official CMU guided campus tour begins, led by Tartan Ambassadors 9 a.m.–4:30 p.m. daily (closed Wednesdays). Dining in Rohr Commons includes Tazza D'Oro (Pittsburgh-local coffee) and Pure (healthy bowls). In fall 2024, School of Design graduate students installed interactive updates including a miniature version of The Fence and a student-project wall. President Jahanian has called Tepper Quad CMU's "new front door to campus." A fun cultural aside: it's next door to the WQED studios where Mister Rogers' Neighborhood was filmed.
