# TCS Hall

## Overview

TCS Hall opened April 21, 2020 and remains one of CMU's newest academic buildings. Dedicated in a ceremony with Governor Tom Wolf, Tata Sons Chairman N. Chandrasekaran, and President Farnam Jahanian. Funded by a $35 million gift from Tata Consultancy Services in 2015 — at the time, the largest industry and international donation in CMU history — the gift also funds 17+ student fellowships and 20+ capstone projects. Roughly 90,000 sq ft on 5 stories above a ground-floor parking garage, LEED Gold certified, designed by Bohlin Cywinski Jackson (Gregory Mottola and Kent Suhrbier, both CMU architecture alumni). Located at 4615 Forbes Avenue, it's the first major building north of Forbes and serves as CMU's literal front door from Forbes Avenue. The building houses the Institute for Software Research (Software and Societal Systems, S3D) of the School of Computer Science, the Master of Science in Computational Finance (MSCF) program, the University Business Engagement Center, and TCS Pace Port Pittsburgh — TCS's fourth global co-innovation hub (5th floor, 8,000 sq ft), specializing in manufacturing and utilities innovation. Patterned brick, terracotta, and aluminum recall Hornbostel's historic palette in contemporary form. A two-story "Student Collaboration Porch" greets visitors; an outdoor "robot yard" and eco-sustainable rain garden sit alongside. Stephanie Dinkins' 44-foot mural "Good Thoughts, Good Words, Good Deeds" was installed in 2022. The flexible floor plates and drone high-bay make TCS the place where CS students, computational-finance quants, and engineering teams mix.

Major financial firms that recruit from the MSCF program include Citi, Bloomberg, Citadel, Bank of America, Morgan Stanley, Deutsche Bank, Blackrock, AQR, Fidelity, PNC, and BNY Mellon.
