# Porter Hall

## Overview

Porter Hall is the first building constructed on the CMU campus, an original Henry Hornbostel design in his signature Beaux-Arts style and Kittanning cream brick. It connects directly to Baker Hall via a famously long sloped corridor — both buildings were designed with flat sloped corridors instead of stairs so the structures could be converted to factory use if Carnegie Tech had failed as a university.

Today Porter is home to several of CMU's interdisciplinary programs, including the Department of Engineering and Public Policy (EPP) — a uniquely CMU dual-degree program where engineering students pair their major with policy coursework. Porter also houses some of the most heavily used undergraduate lecture halls on campus (rooms commonly numbered Porter A18, A19, and A22), drawing classes from across many departments.
