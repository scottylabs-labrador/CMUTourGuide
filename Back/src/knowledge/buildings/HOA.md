# Hall of the Arts

## Overview

Hall of the Arts (HOA) is a four-story academic building on Carnegie Mellon's campus, located on the Forbes Avenue side near Posner Hall and the rest of the campus arts corridor (Margaret Morrison, Purnell, CFA). Originally designed by Henry Hornbostel, the building's exterior buff-colored brick facade was preserved through its most recent renovation.

The building was originally constructed as the home of the Graduate School of Industrial Administration (GSIA) and most recently housed the Tepper School of Business until Tepper relocated to Tepper Quad in 2018. (Note: Posner Hall, which sits adjacent, was the 1990–1993 GSIA expansion building — not the original. Both housed Tepper before the move.)

A multi-phased gut renovation completed in 2020–2021 transformed the interior into a major facility for the College of Fine Arts. The renovated Hall of the Arts opened in 2020.

The School of Music occupies most of three floors of the four-story building. Denis G. Colwell, Jack G. Buncher Head of the School of Music, called it 'the most significant expansion of space the School of Music has had since it was founded.' Music facilities in HOA include: 13 instrument-dedicated studios, two chamber music rehearsal rooms, three new music classrooms, a music technology room and recording suite, 25 faculty offices (with 50 faculty members now having offices or studios specific to their instrument), and two conference rooms.

The top floor of Hall of the Arts is home to the School of Art's Master of Fine Arts (MFA) Program.

HOA also houses standard registrar-controlled classrooms — notably HOA 107 and HOA 160 — used by classes from across the university and equipped with wireless presenting technology, making it a regular destination for students from many majors taking elective coursework.

Important disambiguation: HOA is distinct from the historic College of Fine Arts (CFA) building, also Hornbostel-designed, on the opposite side of campus. CFA houses the Schools of Architecture, Art, and Music administrative spaces along with the Kresge Theatre and Alumni Concert Hall. If a student's schedule lists 'HOA,' they should head to the building near Posner Hall — not to the CFA building with the famous niches and Great Hall.
