# Walking to the Sky

## Overview

Walking to the Sky is a 100-foot-tall stainless steel sculpture by Jonathan Borofsky (CMU College of Fine Arts, 1964), installed on CMU's campus in May 2006. The tapered pole leans at a 75-degree angle and is roughly the width of two flagpoles. Ten realistically painted life-size figures populate the piece — seven climbing the pole and three standing at its base — representing people of all ages, races, and walks of life ascending together.

Borofsky has said the work was inspired by childhood stories his father told him about a friendly giant living in the sky, and that it celebrates "the human potential for discovering who we are and where we need to go" — all of humanity rising upwards from the earth to the heavens above. The sculpture was donated by CMU trustee Jill Gansman Kraus (A '74) and her husband Peter Kraus. It stands on the lawn at Warner Hall just off Forbes Avenue, and is one of the most photographed spots on campus.
