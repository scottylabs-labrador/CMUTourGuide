# The Fence

## Overview

The Fence is one of CMU's most beloved traditions and is widely considered the most painted object in the world. Originally a wooden footbridge railing built in 1916, it has been continuously painted by student groups for over a century to advertise events, celebrate milestones, and share messages with the community.

Tradition dictates that the Fence can only be painted between midnight and sunrise, by hand using a brush, and a group must be present to guard it 24/7 in order to keep it. By 1993, layers of paint had grown so heavy that the original Fence collapsed under its own weight; the current concrete and steel structure was built to carry the legacy forward.
