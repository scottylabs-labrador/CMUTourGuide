# Warner Hall

## Overview

Warner Hall is home to the Office of Undergraduate Admission on the second floor. Their office is open from 8:30AM - 4:30PM Monday through Friday if you would like to stop in for a walk-in appointment.

The HUB and Enrollment Services also reside here, which would be where you get your Student ID if you choose to attend CMU.

Opened 1965–1966 and named for John Christian Warner, CMU's 4th president (1950–1965), Warner is CMU's main administrative building and sits at 5000 Forbes Avenue — the official mailing address for most of the campus. It houses the Office of the President (currently Farnam Jahanian, CMU's 10th president), the Office of Undergraduate Admission (2nd floor, walk-in M–F 8:30–4:30), the HUB on the lower level (CMU's one-stop enrollment center for financial aid, student accounts, registrar, and ID cards), and most senior administrative offices. A recent Landau Building Company renovation updated 42,011 sq ft across all six stories in under 8 months, bringing restrooms to ADA compliance and fully upgrading HVAC. The quiet treasure inside is the George Nakashima furniture collection — 60 pieces of master-craftsman Nakashima furniture commissioned 1965–1966 by interior designer Paul Planert for Warner's opening, gifted by the Scaife family. Iconic pieces including the Conoid Bench are still in active use in the President's and Admissions offices; it's one of the largest institutional Nakashima collections in the world and is featured on the Bloomberg Connects app. Every prospective student passes through: self-guided tour maps are available in the first-floor vestibules at both Warner and the Coulter Welcome Center, and enrolling students return to The HUB to pick up their CMU Student ID card. Out front on the lawn sits the iconic 100-foot stainless-steel "Walking to the Sky" sculpture by Jonathan Borofsky (CFA '64), installed May 2006 and donated by trustee Jill Gansman Kraus (A '74) and her husband Peter Kraus — a canonical campus photo spot. Fun techie detail: CMU's DNS LOC record geographically points to Warner Hall, making it the "official" location of cmu.edu on the internet.
