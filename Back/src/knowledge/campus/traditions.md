# CMU traditions

## Scottish heritage: Scotty, the tartan, and the Kiltie Band

Andrew Carnegie's Scottish roots run through campus life. The mascot is Scotty, a Scottish Terrier, chosen because the breed embodies confidence, determination, and a lively spirit. The athletic teams are called the Tartans, and the official school colors come from the Carnegie family tartan in blue, red, green, and yellow. The Kiltie Band, the university's marching band, performs in kilts at Homecoming and campus events, and CMU offers one of the only bagpipe performance majors in the country.

## The Fence

The Fence, in the middle of campus, is the campus billboard. Student organizations paint it to advertise events, celebrate milestones, and share messages. The rules are strict: painting happens only between midnight and sunrise, the whole Fence must be painted, and only paintbrushes may be used. A group that wants to keep its message has to guard the Fence in person, because anyone else can repaint it the next night. The Fence began as a wooden footbridge railing in 1916 and has been painted continuously for more than a century.

## Spring Carnival

Spring Carnival dates to 1920 and is one of the oldest traditions at CMU. It is a four-day weekend each April with carnival rides, food, live music, comedy, a talent show, and alumni reunions. The two signature competitions are Booth and Buggy. In Booth, student organizations build elaborate one- or two-story structures along the Midway, each containing a game, all designed around that year's Carnival theme. Buggy, also called Sweepstakes, is the highlight of the weekend and is described in its own entry.

## Homecoming

CMU also celebrates Homecoming each fall, with the Kiltie Band performing and alumni returning to campus.
