# Getting to campus

## Address and drop-off

Carnegie Mellon's main campus is at 5000 Forbes Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15213, in the Oakland neighborhood about 3 miles east of downtown. The most convenient pick-up and drop-off points are on Forbes Avenue in front of the Cohon University Center or Hamburg Hall.

## By bus and from the airport

Pittsburgh Regional Transit (PRT) buses run along Forbes and Fifth Avenues past campus, and CMU students, faculty, and staff ride PRT buses, light rail, and inclines free through the PRT Ready2Ride app (as of April 2026, replacing the ID-card tap). From Pittsburgh International Airport, the 28X Airport Flyer bus runs about every 30 minutes and reaches campus in roughly 40 minutes, stopping on Forbes Avenue.

## Parking

Visitor parking is limited and CMU does not validate parking. The East Campus Garage is the main visitor garage, and the Morewood Lot is free on Saturdays and Sundays. Metered street parking is available on nearby streets. Current rates and validation tickets are handled by Transportation Services, reachable at 412-268-2052 or transportation@andrew.cmu.edu. Many nearby hotels run shuttles to campus, which the university encourages because of the parking shortage.
