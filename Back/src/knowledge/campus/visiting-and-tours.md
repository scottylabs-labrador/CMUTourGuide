# Visiting CMU

## Campus tours and information sessions

Official campus tours and admission information sessions are booked through the online calendar on the CMU campus tours page. A standard tour lasts about one hour, and department-specific tours run 30 minutes to 2 hours and are arranged by request with the department. Live-streamed tours and online information sessions are offered for visitors who cannot come in person, and 360-degree virtual tours of academic buildings and student life spaces are available online at any time. Admission counselors are not available for one-on-one meetings on Saturdays or Sundays.

## Where to start

Visitors begin at the Coulter Welcome Center inside the Tepper Quad on the north side of Forbes Avenue. It is open 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. daily except Wednesdays. Printed campus maps are available in the vestibules of the Welcome Center and of Warner Hall. The Office of Undergraduate Admission is on the second floor of Warner Hall at 5000 Forbes Avenue, and its phone number is 412-268-2082.

## Practical notes

CMU does not validate parking for visitors, so plan parking in advance. Luggage storage is not available on campus for security reasons. Tartan Preview Day, a large fall open house for prospective students, is held on a Saturday in late September.
