# CMU and Pittsburgh

## The neighborhood

CMU sits in Oakland, Pittsburgh's academic and cultural hub, about 3 miles east of downtown and next door to the University of Pittsburgh, the Carnegie Museums of Art and Natural History, and the Carnegie Library. Schenley Park, with its trails, fields, and Phipps Conservatory, is directly behind campus, and Pittsburgh has about 3,800 acres of city parks in total. Upperclass and graduate students often live in the surrounding neighborhoods: Squirrel Hill, known for its restaurants, and Shadyside, known for tree-lined streets and Walnut Street shops.

## Free museums with the Arts Pass

CMU students get free admission to eight cultural venues through the CMU Arts Pass: the Carnegie Museum of Art, the Carnegie Museum of Natural History, the Andy Warhol Museum, the Carnegie Science Center, the Heinz History Center, the Mattress Factory, the National Aviary, and Phipps Conservatory and Botanical Gardens.

## The city

Pittsburgh has grown into a technology center, with nearly 300,000 people working in tech industries and major offices for Google, Uber, Microsoft, and Apple, many of them clustered near campus because of CMU research. Startups with CMU roots include Duolingo. The cost of living is around the national average and rents run about 15 percent below it, and the city ranks highly for graduates finding jobs. Popular student activities include hiking and biking in the parks, kayaking on the three rivers, quarterly Gallery Crawls in the Cultural District, and the city's sports teams.
