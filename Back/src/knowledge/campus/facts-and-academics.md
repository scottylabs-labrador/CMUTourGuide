# CMU at a glance

## Size and people

Carnegie Mellon enrolls about 16,600 students from more than 130 countries, with roughly 7,800 undergraduates. There are about 1,600 faculty from more than 60 countries, and the student-to-faculty ratio is 6 to 1. The university has more than 127,000 living alumni in over 150 countries. The main campus is about 122 acres in the Oakland neighborhood of Pittsburgh, roughly 3 miles from downtown, with additional campuses in Silicon Valley, Qatar, and Rwanda (CMU-Africa). The official address is 5000 Forbes Avenue, Pittsburgh, PA 15213. CMU's motto is "My heart is in the work," and its tagline is "Don't envision the future, invent it every day."

## The seven schools and colleges

CMU is organized into seven schools and colleges: the College of Engineering, the College of Fine Arts, the Dietrich College of Humanities and Social Sciences, the Heinz College of Information Systems and Public Policy, the Mellon College of Science, the School of Computer Science, and the Tepper School of Business. The university emphasizes work across disciplines rather than within silos, and many degree programs are jointly run by two or more colleges.

## Rankings and best-known majors

CMU is consistently ranked among the top 20 universities in the United States. Its best-known majors and programs are in computer science and engineering: it is ranked number one nationally in seven undergraduate programs, including artificial intelligence, programming languages, cybersecurity, software engineering, information systems, analytics, and mobile and web applications. The School of Drama and the College of Fine Arts are also nationally prominent, reflected in the alumni award counts below.

## Awards and honors

CMU faculty and alumni include 21 Nobel laureates across chemistry, economics, peace, physics, and medicine, and 13 winners of the Turing Award, computing's highest honor. Alumni have won at least 142 Emmy Awards, 64 Tony Awards, and 14 Academy Awards, reflecting the strength of the School of Drama and the College of Fine Arts. The community also counts dozens of members of the National Academies of Sciences and Engineering, and a 2025 Pulitzer Prize in History was awarded to a CMU professor.
