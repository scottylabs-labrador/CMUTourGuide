# Where to eat on campus

## How dining works at CMU

CMU has no single central dining hall. Instead there are more than 30 eateries spread across campus buildings, most of them small cafés and counters, plus one all-you-care-to-eat buffet. Hours change by semester and are shorter on weekends and in summer, so check the CMU Dining site or the Grubhub app, which many locations use for pick-up orders. Dining Services is based at Residence on Fifth, 4700 Fifth Avenue, and can be reached at 412-268-3782.

## Cohon University Center

The Cohon University Center has the deepest lineup on campus. The Schatz Dining Room on the second floor is the only all-you-care-to-eat buffet. The first floor has Au Bon Pain at Skibo Café, El Gallo de Oro for Mexican food, and Revolution Noodle, which opened in 2022 with build-your-own Malatang noodle bowls, bao buns, and bubble tea.

## Tepper Quad

Rohr Commons inside the Tepper Quad is the other big food hub, with Build Pizza, Forbes Ave Subs, Tepper Taqueria, Millie's Coffee 'N Creamery, Tazza D'Oro coffee, and Pure for healthy bowls.

## Cafés in academic buildings

La Prima Espresso in the Wean Hall lobby has been a campus staple for more than 30 years and is Pittsburgh's only certified organic coffee roaster; it also has a location in the Gates and Hillman Centers. Rohr Café on the third floor of Gates serves espresso and sandwiches and has one of the healthier menus on campus. The Maggie Murph Café on the first floor of Hunt Library is run by De Fer Coffee & Tea, a roastery founded by a CMU alumnus. Redhawk Coffee is on the first floor of Scaife Hall. Ginger's Express is on the lower level of Baker Hall and in Purnell. The Exchange on the first floor of Posner Hall is a 20-year institution known for custom deli sandwiches, especially "The Leonardo," scratch-made soups, and a salad bar. Rothberg's Roasters is in Scott Hall, and Stephanie's is in the Mellon Institute.

## Near the athletic buildings

Shake Smart in the Highmark Center serves protein shakes, smoothies, and açaí bowls. The Carnegie Mellon Café and the Tartans Pavilion are next to Gesling Stadium.
