# CMU Explorer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

CMU Explorer ("the app") is a self-guided campus tour app for Carnegie Mellon University, built by students in ScottyLabs with the CMU Welcome Center. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how long we keep it.

## What we collect

**Photos you scan.** When you point the camera at a building and tap scan, the photo is sent to our server to identify the building. We keep a copy of scanned photos to improve the recognition model. Photos are not linked to your name or account, and we do not access your photo library.

**Chat messages.** Questions you type in the chat are sent to our server, which uses a third-party language model (through OpenRouter) to generate answers. Chat answers are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Messages and replies are logged with a random device identifier so we can review answer quality. They are not linked to your name. Please do not include personal information in your messages. Your chat history in the app is stored only on your device.

**Location.** With your permission, the app uses your location to show where you are on the campus map. To draw a walking route, the app sends coordinates to our routing provider (OpenRouteService). We do not store your location history.

**Usage analytics.** We use PostHog to understand how the app is used, such as which screens are opened, which buildings are scanned, and whether scans succeed. This is tied to a random device identifier, not to you.

**Feedback.** If you send feedback through the app, the message, its category, and your device platform are sent to our team's private Discord channel. Do not include personal information you don't want us to see.

## What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or any account information. There are no accounts or logins in the app. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Third parties

- **OpenRouter** and the language model provider it routes to process chat messages to generate replies.
- **PostHog** stores usage analytics and chat logs.
- **Modal** runs the building recognition model on scanned photos.
- **Amazon Web Services (S3)** stores scanned photos.
- **OpenRouteService** computes walking routes from coordinates the app sends.
- **Google Maps** displays the campus map. Google may receive map requests from your device, as described in Google's privacy policy.
- **Discord** delivers in-app feedback to our team.

Each service processes data only to provide its part of the app.

## How long we keep data

We keep scanned photos, chat logs, and analytics only as long as they are useful for improving the app, and we delete them when they are no longer needed. Location coordinates sent for routing are not stored by us.

## Your choices

- Deny camera or location permission in iOS Settings. The app works without them, with reduced features.
- Use "Clear All Progress" on the About screen to erase progress stored on your device.
- Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.
- To ask us to delete data collected from your device, email us at the address below. Because we do not collect names, include the approximate date and time you used the app so we can find it.

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience of campus visitors and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
