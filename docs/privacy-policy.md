# CMU Explorer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2, 2026_

CMU Explorer ("the app") is a campus tour guide for Carnegie Mellon University, built by ScottyLabs. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and where it goes.

## What we collect

**Photos you scan.** When you point the camera at a building and tap scan, the photo is sent to our server to identify the building. We keep a copy of scanned photos to improve the recognition model. Photos are not linked to your name or account, and we do not collect photos from your camera roll.

**Chat messages.** Questions you type in the chat are sent to our server, which uses a third-party language model (via OpenRouter) to generate answers. Messages are not linked to your identity. Your chat history is stored only on your device.

**Location.** With your permission, the app uses your location to show where you are on the campus map and to draw walking routes. Your location is used on the device and for route requests to our routing provider (OpenRouteService). We do not store your location history.

**Usage analytics.** We use PostHog to understand how the app is used: which screens are opened, which buildings are scanned, and whether scans succeed. This is tied to a random device identifier, not to you. We do not collect your name, email, or contacts.

**Feedback.** If you send feedback through the app, the message, its category, and your device platform are relayed to our team. Do not include personal information you don't want us to see.

## What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, or any account information. There are no accounts or logins in the app.

## Third parties

- **OpenRouter** processes chat messages to generate replies.
- **PostHog** stores usage analytics.
- **Amazon Web Services** stores scanned photos.
- **OpenRouteService** computes walking routes from coordinates you send.

Each processes data only to provide its part of the service.

## Your choices

- Deny camera or location permission in iOS Settings; the app works without them, with reduced features.
- Use "Clear All Progress" on the About screen to erase unlocked buildings stored on your device.
- Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
